and why it works, so I won't seek to improve on that.
However, what's often absent is just how awesome and useful the GCD actually is:  Literally one of Math's powertools.

<h2>Detective Euclid cracks another case</h2>
<img src="/sloi/img/fact/gcd/gcd2.jpg" />


It seems amazing to me, how factoring is such a difficult problem. (<a href="https://www.claymath.org/millennium/p-vs-np/">Citation needed</a>),
With the number unwilling to divulge any non-trivial factors.  But, simply ask about another number via the GCD, and it'll happily spill the beans the 
largest factor it might have in common.
